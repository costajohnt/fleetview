# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the work.
Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning comments are not welcome.

fleetview is maintained by one person. Report conduct concerns to
**costajohnt@gmail.com**; reports are handled privately and in good faith.
